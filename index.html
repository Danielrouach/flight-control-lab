LONGITUDINAL DYNAMICS

α̇ = q + Zα/(mV₀)·α + Zδ/(mV₀)·δ
q̇ = Mα/Iyy·α + Mq/Iyy·q + Mδ/Iyy·δ
δ̇ = (δc − δ)/τδ
az = Zα/m·α + Zδ/m·δ

OBSERVER + INTEGRAL CONTROLLER

δc = sat(−K·x̂ − ki·ξ, ±10°)
x̂̇ = A·x̂ + B·δc + L(y − Co·x̂)
ξ̇ = r − az
y = [az, q]ᵀ

State vector x = [α, q, δ]ᵀ. The observer uses a nominal command; an applied actuator-input disturbance is unknown to it. Pitch θ is reconstructed by integrating q with θ(0)=0. The displayed model is linearized about trim, not a full six-degree-of-freedom flight model.

RELATIVE GEOMETRY

R = √(rx² + rh²)
λ = atan2(rh, rx)
λ̇ = (rx·vrh − rh·vrx)/R²
Vc = −(rx·vrx + rh·vrh)/R

GUIDANCE & ACTUATOR LAG

aPN = N·max(Vc, 0)·λ̇
ac = sat(aPN + g·cos(γ), ±2g)
γ̇ = (aactual − g·cos(γ))/vM
ȧactual = (ac − aactual)/τa

Speed vM is assumed constant, target motion is horizontal and constant-velocity, and the lagged model initializes acceleration at g (level-flight equilibrium). The first-order lag is not the complete longitudinal autopilot from Experiment 01.

Engineering Scope & Limitations

01 / OPERATING POINT

Part 1 linearized around 100 m/s. Part 2 assumes constant 50 m/s by default. No end-to-end airframe coupling is implied.

02 / SIGN CONVENTIONS

Autopilot IMU az is positive body-down. Interception normal acceleration is positive upward; the two are not directly interchangeable.

03 / CONTROLLER DESIGN

Continuous-time LQE gains and pole-placement gains are sourced from MATLAB. Process noise covariance is a design assumption.

04 / INTEGRATION

The browser uses deterministic fixed-step classical RK4 and bisection of the 5 m terminal event. MATLAB uses adaptive ode45 with tight tolerances.
